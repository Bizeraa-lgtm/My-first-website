Introduction to psychology

The basic functions of the brain

The human brain never ceases to amaze us with its complexity and functions. Psychology revolves around studying the mental processes of it that lead to diverse behaviours, either moral or not as healthy. Some of the few most basic brain functions and also the most important ones are:

  • Perception
  • Motivation
  • Cognition
  • Self regulation
  • Learning and memory
  1. Our perception varies, being one of the unique things that each and every person has. It involves the way we perceive the outside world, be it in a positive, negative or neutral light, depending on the way we grew up.
  2. Motivation represents our drive to do something specifically, usually for the dopamine hit we receive from it directly or for our self improvement, changing the way we perceive our own person.
  3. The cognitive functionrepresents the way we think and the overall "engine" of our thoughts. It can be affected by diverse events, but a group of people can have the same thought process.
  4. Self regulationinvolves our ability of -just the way it sounds- regulating our emotions or feelings, being an essential brain function in our day to day life in the cotidian world.
  5. Learning and memory. This process never ceases to expand, although we get most of our informations about the world up until age 7. Our memory evolves during teenage and adult years, but we tend to lose parts of it during our latest phase of life, which is the "wrinkled" one.

    6. Why do we need to know all of this?

    Knowing the basics of psychology, a little bit of biology and some neurology is the key to understanding your own person and improve your way of living, your beliefs and even your perception about the outside world.
